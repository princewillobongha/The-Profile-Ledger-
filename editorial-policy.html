EDITORIAL POLICY

How The Profile Ledger handles biographies and personal-life claims

We distinguish established facts from claims and allegations. A statement about a public figure's private life is not presented as confirmed unless the available evidence supports it.

Corrections

When credible evidence changes a factual statement, the relevant article should be updated and the basis for the update identified.

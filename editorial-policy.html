EDITORIAL POLICY

How we handle profiles and rumors

Our profiles are written for readers seeking reliable public information. We use identifiable sources where possible and date time-sensitive information.

Rumors

We may discuss a public rumor when it is itself newsworthy, but we label it as a claim and do not present it as established fact without credible supporting evidence.

Corrections

If reliable evidence shows that a factual statement is wrong, the article should be corrected and the correction noted when appropriate.

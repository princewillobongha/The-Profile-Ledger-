Who is Dr. Janette Nesheiwat?

Dr. Janette Nesheiwat is a physician who became widely known through medical commentary and public-health work. Fox News describes her as a double-board-certified physician and reported in May 2026 that she was working at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center caring for members of the U.S. military and veterans.

Education and medical career

Public reporting says Nesheiwat studied at the American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine and completed family-medicine residency training through the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences' Area Health Education Center, where she was selected as chief resident. Fox News has also reported that she received board certification in family medicine in 2009 and urgent care medicine in 2020.

Media and public-health work

Nesheiwat became a Fox News medical contributor during the COVID-19 pandemic and appeared in coverage discussing medical and public-health issues. Reporting also describes disaster-relief work, including involvement with Samaritan's Purse disaster assistance teams.

Surgeon General nomination

In November 2024, President-elect Donald Trump announced her nomination for U.S. Surgeon General. That nomination was later withdrawn in 2025, after which Fox News reported that Casey Means was nominated instead. This site describes the nomination as a historical event rather than a current office held by Nesheiwat.

For that reason, this publication will not state as an established fact that Dr. Nesheiwat is legally married to Roberto Uy/Urbina, nor will it label her single as a verified fact. Why this is separated: relationship rumors can easily become misinformation. A publication can report that a claim exists without presenting the claim itself as proof.

Her sisters Julia and Jaclyn are also public figures. Family details are included here only where they have been publicly documented.

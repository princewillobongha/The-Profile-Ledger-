Who is Dr. Janette Nesheiwat?

Dr. Janette Nesheiwat is an American physician who became known nationally through medical commentary and public-health communication. Her professional biography describes work in family and urgent-care medicine, medical missions and disaster response, and media appearances. Her publisher also describes her as a Family and Emergency Medicine doctor and author of Beyond the Stethoscope: Miracles in Medicine.

Janette Nesheiwat age

Public biographical records list her birth date as August 25, 1976, which places her at 50 years old in September 2026. Age information can change only with the passage of time; the birth date is the underlying fact used for this calculation.

Janette Nesheiwat education

Available biographies report that Nesheiwat earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of South Florida in 2000 and later earned her medical degree from the American University of the Caribbean School of Medicine. She completed family-medicine residency training through the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences, where her biography says she served as chief resident.

Janette Nesheiwat medical career

Her career has included clinical medicine, urgent care and emergency medicine, medical leadership, disaster-relief missions and national medical commentary. Her publisher's biography says she has worked in emergency care and led or participated in medical relief missions. In May 2026, Fox News reported that she took a role at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center caring for military personnel and veterans and working with patients experiencing anomalous health incidents.

Janette Nesheiwat family

Nesheiwat has publicly discussed being one of five children raised by her widowed mother, Hayat Nesheiwat, a pediatric nurse. Public biographies identify her sisters Julia Nesheiwat and Jaclyn Stapp among her siblings.

Janette Nesheiwat achievements

Her documented professional achievements include community-service recognition in Arkansas, selection for Arkansas Business's 40 Under 40 in 2013, medical-relief work and national medical-media work. She also authored Beyond the Stethoscope: Miracles in Medicine. In 2024 she was nominated for U.S. Surgeon General; the nomination was subsequently withdrawn.

## Important before launch
The sitemap and robots.txt use a placeholder domain. After you know the exact live website URL, replace `https://https://the-profile-ledger.vercel.app/` in `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` with the real URL. A correct absolute canonical domain can also be added once the live URL is known.

Submitting the sitemap in Google Search Console can help Google discover the pages, but indexing and search ranking are determined by Google and are not guaranteed.
